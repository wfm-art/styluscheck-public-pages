Classroom Resource Management

Personal, non-commercial educational application

StylusCheck is developed by a teacher to organize and track classroom equipment, including styluses and Chromebooks.

It records resource identities, availability, assignments, checkouts and returns for everyday classroom use.

Personal educational use.

StylusCheck is not sold, has no advertising and does not sell data. It is not a publicly offered service.

Access to operational information is restricted to authorized users. These information pages are separate from the application and are not required to use it.

For information about data handling, see the Privacy Policy.
